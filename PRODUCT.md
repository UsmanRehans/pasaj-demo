# Product

## Platform

Web.

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript replica with a Node development server and Vercel static hosting. This is a reversible implementation choice by the coordinating assistant, not an explicit owner framework preference. A static versus Next.js question was offered; no answer was received before proceeding.

## Users

Visitors browsing PASAJ fragrance, skincare and home fragrance. A design-conscious, ritual-oriented audience is a research interpretation, not verified customer demographics.

## Product Purpose

Reproduce the existing https://www.pasajstudio.com/ site using its original photos on Vercel for now. Also provide durable project team skills and a source-backed Markdown brand book.

## Positioning

Brand-stated emphasis on natural materials and daily rituals. The working interpretation is restrained, material-led luxury. See the evidence labels in `docs/brand/README.md`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Public pages, source images, navigation and responsive source styling; local cart and catalog search; source product variant data captured September 21, 2026. Real checkout/account and form submission depend on original PASAJ services. This is not a migrated Shopify backend. No production domain change or real order was requested.

## Brand Commitments

Owner explicitly requested faithful replication and original photos. Preserve the PASAJ wordmark, source layout, monochrome monospace interface, copy and product identity. Impeccable and the relevant Candy Rama team process are installed; Candy Rama brand facts do not apply.

## Evidence on Hand

Public source HTML/CSS, product JSON, sitemap inventory, asset manifest, Avery’s content audit and Vita’s actual desktop/mobile observations. Extended brand synthesis is by the coordinating assistant following specialist usage-limit failures. No official manual, claims dossier or analytics was provided.
