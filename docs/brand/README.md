# Understanding PASAJ

Prepared September 21, 2026 from the public PASAJ storefront. This is a source-based working brand book, not a claim that we received an official brand manual. Start here to follow the reasoning rather than just the conclusions.

1. [Brand foundations](foundations.md): what the studio says about itself, its origin and product world.
2. [Voice and editorial guide](voice.md): how the language works, examples and boundaries.
3. [Colors and typography](colors-and-typography.md): exact observed interface tokens and their application.
4. [Luxury positioning](luxury-positioning.md): the design and language signals behind the interpretation.
5. [Photography and art direction](photography.md): source images, materials, crops and atmosphere.
6. [Products and materials](products-and-materials.md): catalog scope, formulation claims and commercial boundaries.
7. [Customer experience](customer-experience.md): discovery, shopping, trust and service expectations.
8. [Research sources and method](research-and-sources.md): evidence trail, certainty, limitations and open questions.
9. [Decisions and learning](decisions.md): dated owner instructions and implementation decisions.

**How to read the evidence:** “Observed” means visible on the site or in its CSS. “Brand-stated” means PASAJ says it; we have not independently substantiated it. “Interpretation” means our reasoning from those observations. “Proposed” means advice or draft language that the owner has not approved.

The independent research completed this session consists of Avery’s [content inventory](../content-audit.md) and Vita’s [visual audit](../visual-audit.md). The extended brand-book synthesis was completed by the coordinating assistant after specialist follow-ups hit a usage limit. It has not yet received Lauren’s independent editorial sign-off.
