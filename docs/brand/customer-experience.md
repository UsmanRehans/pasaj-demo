# Customer experience and operational truth

This maps the visible storefront, not a verified merchant operations manual. Nico’s persistent role owns future operational review; no independent Nico review ran this session.

## Journey

Visitors can start with a product category, a gift collection, Curated Essentials or the homepage’s editorial imagery. Collection pages expose availability/price controls and sorting. Product pages support imagery, size choice, quantity and cart. About and Materials provide context; policies and contact supply service information.

## Service expectations

Preserve the source policy text and direct links, but do not infer fulfillment speed, shipping territories or return exceptions from photography or tone. Operational facts require current policy and merchant verification. Country/currency is a real commerce setting, not decorative interface text.

## Preview behavior

The local cart is stored in the visitor’s browser. Checkout hands chosen variant IDs and quantities to the original PASAJ store. Real account access remains on PASAJ. The preview should not collect messages or subscriptions unless connected to an authorized backend, and should never display false success.

Source apps such as chat, newsletter popups, subscriptions, ratings and carbon widgets may depend on Shopify extension code. Reproducing the static page does not reproduce those services. Record omissions and handoffs in the release notes.

## Trust priorities

Accurate variants and availability; consistent cart totals; visible destination for checkout; accessible menu/drawer controls; useful empty states; no hidden form submission to unrelated services; and no accidental implication that the replica is the live production store.
