# PASAJ brand foundations

Evidence captured September 21, 2026. Source: [About](https://www.pasajstudio.com/pages/about-new), [Materials](https://www.pasajstudio.com/pages/materials-in-progress), [homepage](https://www.pasajstudio.com/).

## What the studio says

The About page says PASAJ was founded in 2018 by Tarab and Tabish. Its name takes inspiration from a historic Istanbul arcade once lined with florists. The region’s cultural and artisanal history is a reference for the studio. This supports a cultural connection; it does not establish that every product is made in Istanbul, or identify the founders’ nationality.

The studio works across perfume, skincare and home fragrance. The About page presents these as objects for daily ritual, grounded in natural materials and repeated use. The Materials page emphasizes botanical character, seasonal variation and the relationships between ingredients.

## Working interpretation

PASAJ offers an intimate, deliberate encounter with scent and body care. The most defensible brand territory is **quiet presence through material and ritual**. This phrase is our synthesis, not an official slogan.

The recurring ideas are presence, material character, personal rhythm, intention and restraint. The site’s journey moves between bodies, landscape and product still lifes. Its visual expression makes small everyday actions feel considered.

## Audience hypothesis

The presentation appears intended for shoppers interested in natural fragrance, sensory detail, thoughtful gifts and restrained design. This is inferred from products and creative presentation. We have no customer interviews, analytics, demographic profile, income data or evidence of conversion performance. Do not describe an affluent age group or gender as proven target-market fact.

## What must survive the rebuild

The PASAJ name and original wordmark; actual product names and source photography; current section sequence; monochrome interface; sensory but measured language; material specificity; source product notes, rituals and compositions. The owner requested a total visual replica, not a new identity.

## What remains unknown

An official brand strategy, logo usage manual, certified claims dossier, manufacturing locations, sourcing verification, customer research, business performance, and long-term Shopify/Vercel integration plan have not been supplied. Capture gaps as questions for future work, not invented answers.
