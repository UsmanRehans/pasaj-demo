# How PASAJ expresses luxury

This is an interpretation of the current site, not proof of customer perception or a claim that PASAJ has adopted a formal “luxury” positioning document.

## Quiet, intimate, material-led

The original site invests visual space in a small product universe. Large photographs let a bottle, a surface or a gesture carry attention. The interface is nearly monochrome. Copy dwells on scent, texture, memory and the rhythms of use. Together these create a sense of deliberateness and intimacy.

The About page’s Istanbul arcade reference supplies cultural context. The Materials page supplies a language of botanical character and variation. Product pages add specific scent notes and full compositions. These details give the atmospheric presentation something concrete to rest on.

## Evidence → interpretation → design implication

| Evidence | Interpretation | Implication for faithful work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large full-bleed hero and alternating generous image/text sections | The visitor is invited to slow down | Preserve whitespace, section rhythm and photographic scale |
| Restrained monospace interface and bold image wordmark | A precise studio identity | Keep the observed type rather than adding luxury clichés |
| Memory-like fragrance descriptions | Emotional value comes from personal experience | Use sensory specificity rather than price/status comparisons |
| Notes, Ritual and Composition sections | Craft is expressed through considered detail | Retain product information and clear buying controls |
| Natural materials and seasonal variation in brand language | Materials are a point of view | Preserve nuance; do not invent certifications |
| Curated gifts and collection discovery | Selection feels considered | Keep the editorial collection architecture |

## What the site does not prove

The creative does not establish artisanal production capacity, hand-making methods, exclusive supply chains, sustainability certifications, ingredient safety or superior performance. Prices alone do not prove luxury positioning. We have no willingness-to-pay study, conversion analysis or customer interviews.

## What would weaken this identity

Unrequested gold accents; decorative serif headlines; crowded promotional banners; inflated superlatives; invented heritage; excessive badges; uniformly bright marketplace product grids replacing atmospheric scenes; or a mobile layout compressed until the pacing disappears.

## Commercial balance

Luxury atmosphere should not hide price, size, availability, ingredients or service information. The source already separates storytelling from practical product detail. Preserve that distinction. The current preview must accurately disclose when checkout or a service continues on the original store.
