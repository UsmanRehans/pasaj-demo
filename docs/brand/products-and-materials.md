# Product universe and materials

Snapshot: September 21, 2026. See `public/catalog.json` for exact public product/variant records, and `docs/route-inventory.json` for source URLs.

## Range

Avery found 14 public products spanning four perfume oils, four candles, and six skin/hair/body care products. The navigation groups Perfume, Face, Body, Hair, Candles and Gifts. Collections also organize gifts and curated essentials. Match visible product titles rather than deriving names from older route slugs: Gardenya and Oud route names can differ from displayed Gardeniah and Ooude names.

## Product-page structure

Photography and product name lead into sensory description, size, price and availability. Expandable sections contain scent notes, ritual and composition where applicable. This structure combines emotional discovery with ingredient specificity.

Observed Rose Ottoman sizes are 5ml and 10ml; the selected source page displayed $45 USD. The perfume collection displayed a range up to $78. These are dated observations, not permanent pricing promises. The current source skincare collection showed some sold-out products. Preserve availability from captured variant records; do not make every product purchasable for visual convenience.

## Brand-stated formulation position

The Materials page says the studio works exclusively with natural materials, including botanicals, woods, resins, extracts and essential oils. It says formulations exclude synthetic fragrance, parabens and phthalates and describes sourcing through growers, distillers and suppliers selected for material quality and their approach to land.

These are PASAJ’s public statements. This research did not inspect certificates, lab reports, supplier contracts, manufacturing records or safety assessments. Do not convert the language into independent verification or broader claims such as universal allergy safety.

## Sustainability

The Climate Commitment page describes contributions based on estimated shipping emissions to carbon-removal businesses and names Heirloom, Remora and Charm. Its aggregate counters contain awkward generic wording. This documents the source’s published program description; it is not an audit of actual donations, current partner relationships or delivered carbon-removal quantities.

## Implementation boundary

The Vercel build is a snapshot. Local cart state does not reserve stock. Prices and availability can change on the original Shopify store. The original store is authoritative at checkout. Account, newsletter, contact, localization and subscription services require their real backend or an explicit handoff.
