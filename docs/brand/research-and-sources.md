# Research method and sources

Research date: September 21, 2026.

## Method

1. Avery inspected the public homepage and XML sitemaps, recording disclosed routes in `docs/route-inventory.json`.
2. Vita inspected actual rendered desktop 1280×720 and mobile 390×844 views, recording geometry, typography, image identity, overlays and menus in `docs/visual-audit.md`.
3. The coordinating assistant inspected the HTML/CSS, extracted exact theme tokens, read the main content of About, Materials, Climate Commitment, representative products and collections, and obtained public product JSON.
4. The extended brand book translates these observations into labeled interpretations and future writing/design rules. It is not an official brand manual or an independent claims audit.
5. The public capture script stores original image provenance and records failures instead of silently substituting assets.

## Confidence

High confidence: observed page structure, source filenames, CSS values, product records at capture time, exact published statements. Medium confidence: creative interpretation of restraint, ritual and material-led luxury. Unverified: audience demographics, business results, causal conversion impact, manufacturing, certifications, safety and environmental performance.

## Limits and next evidence

No Shopify admin access, customer research, analytics, official brand manual, supplier evidence or claims dossier was used. Source pages can change. Future owner corrections override working interpretations and should be recorded in `decisions.md` with date and reason.

Team execution was real for the first inventory and visual audit. Specialist follow-ups hit the account usage limit. Further synthesis and integration were completed by the coordinating assistant, without invented specialist consensus.
