## Voice in one sentence

Interpretation: calm, sensory and precise, with the intimacy of a studio note rather than the insistence of a sales pitch.

## Tone by surface

Homepage: compact, spacious, welcoming. Collection: clarify what unites the range. Product: sensory opening followed by usable notes, size, price, ritual and composition. Materials: lucid and specific. Support: plain and direct; shipping and return explanations should not be poetic. Errors: name the problem and recovery without blame.

## Vocabulary

Observed themes include natural materials, botanicals, woods, resins, composition, character, variation, skin, ritual and presence. “Studio” helps unify scent, care and home objects. Use these when they actually clarify the product; a paragraph overloaded with atmospheric nouns loses precision.

Avoid unsubstantiated superlatives, urgency tactics, invented scarcity, prestige clichés, clinical promises, and broad “safe for everyone” statements. Do not add “organic,” “hypoallergenic,” “dermatologist-approved,” “non-toxic,” or “sustainably sourced” unless supporting evidence is provided.

## Mechanics

Use short paragraphs and sentence-case headings. Product names keep their exact published spelling. Keep prices, size units and ingredients precise. Allow a lyrical fragment in product storytelling; preserve complete and unambiguous sentences in service copy. Direct links and restrained CTAs are consistent with the source.

## Proposed examples — not published source copy

A fitting studio note: “A small ritual for the end of the day. Warm a few drops between your palms, then take your time.” This is tone illustration only; application instructions still need the actual product directions.

Too loud: “The ultimate luxury essential you need right now.” It replaces material specificity with assertion and pressure.

A fitting error: “We couldn’t send your message. Please try again.” On the current replica, do not use that error to imply a connected message service. Say where the real form can be completed.

## Claims tension to preserve in the research

The homepage makes a broad allergen-free statement while product ingredient lists include many botanicals and essential oils. The site is evidence of what the brand claims, not proof of safety for every person. The replica retains source content; future copy should undergo substantiation review before expanding these statements.
