# PASAJ marketing questions for later

Ask these only when the related work needs the answer. Current scope is USA skincare across Meta Ads, Shopify and Shopify native email. The monthly Meta budget is $1,000 USD for ad spend only. Shopify native email is the current platform.

## Before building the first campaign

- Which skincare product or products should lead? Are there stock, margin, shipping, return or claim constraints?
- What outcome and reporting window should the first campaign target? Are there recent Shopify orders/refunds or Meta campaign results we can use as a baseline?
- What campaign assets are approved and available? Should production/creative planning happen separately from the Meta media budget?
- Is the $1,000 ad budget intended as a monthly cap, and should it run continuously or in defined campaigns?

## Before configuring email

- Which existing Shopify email campaigns or flows are active, and what results or customer feedback are available?
- What customer groups, consent rules and sending cadence should apply?

## Before publishing or activating

- Who has final approval for ad creative, claims, audiences and spend changes?
- Who has final approval for email copy, segments and sends?

Do not request credentials in this document. Verify live account access through the available authorized tools when a task requires it.
