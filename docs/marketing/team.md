# PASAJ marketing structure

The source library organizes skills around shared product context and seven capability groups. PASAJ keeps that structure and assigns work to its existing agent roles. These assignments are implementation choices, open to owner revision. Roles operate during requested tasks; they are not continuously running employees.

Commercial focus: **USA skincare**, **$1,000 USD monthly marketing budget**, and **online sales plus repeat purchases**. Read the product context for remaining gaps. Budget allocation is not yet set; the full amount is not automatically a Meta ad-spend allowance.

Current owner-confirmed scope: **Meta Ads, Shopify and email**. Retain all seven groups for later work. Cando coordinates Meta campaigns with Avery on measurement and Vita/Lauren on creative. Read `.agents/product-marketing.md`, `docs/brand/README.md`, `docs/brand/decisions.md` and the relevant role contracts in `docs/team/`. Canonical brand evidence stays in the brand book. Use the product context to track marketing objectives, commercial constraints and unresolved questions.

## Conditional capabilities

- `onboarding` and `churn-prevention`: adapt useful ideas to product education, replenishment and repeat orders. Subscription cancellation/dunning applies only if an actual subscription business is confirmed.
- `paywalls`, `aso`, `directory-submissions`: retained in the reference library; no current PASAJ use case established. Do not invent an app, software paywall or software-directory campaign.
- `marketing-council`: source is an explicitly simulated famous-marketer council. The `copy-editing` playbook also prescribes persona panels. PASAJ uses actual delegated specialist reviews in both cases. Frameworks may be discussed with attribution; do not portray simulations as independent reviews.
- `marketing-loops`: available for a future explicit recurring-work request. Define data access, cadence, actions, state, budget limits and stop conditions then, using the app’s supported automation tools.
- Zahid joins scoped security/privacy work when necessary. Miles is not assigned without an Amazon brief.

## Work cycle

First priority, confirmed September 28, 2026: **online sales and repeat purchases**. Start with available purchase/retention evidence, the real purchase journey and relevant email lifecycle opportunities. Plan Meta acquisition once markets, economics and budget are known. Other channels and wholesale are deferred.

1. Cando identifies the requested outcome, accountable role, needed evidence and deliverable. Read saved answers before asking questions. Continue useful work while material gaps remain open.
2. Delegate bounded independent work when useful, passing PASAJ context and non-overlapping file ownership. Identify actual specialists and report which reviews actually ran.
3. Load the relevant source skills. Translate SaaS examples into physical-product economics and journeys. Reject tactics that conflict with the brief or lack evidence.
4. Produce a concrete artifact. For a campaign: objective, audience status, product/stock facts, channel, message, assets, destination, budget if known, metric and next decision. For research: sources, dates, findings and uncertainty.
5. Bring in relevant review: Lauren for copy/claims, Vita for creative fidelity, Nico for stock/service/offer truth, Avery for measurement, Theo for technical changes. Not every task needs every reviewer.
6. Complete authorized work and verification. Drafting does not itself authorize sending messages, buying ads, publishing campaigns or scheduling future runs. Honor explicit authorization already given without repeatedly requesting it.
7. Record owner decisions in `docs/brand/decisions.md` and update the versioned product context when facts change. Label proposed tactics and untested hypotheses.

## PASAJ adaptations

- Preserve the restrained, sensory voice and exact product/packaging references. Do not import Candy Rama commercial facts or aesthetics.
- The website remains a faithful replica unless the owner changes that brief. Marketing proposals do not silently authorize a redesign. Apply Impeccable when actual interface work is requested.
- Prior ad-generation approvals are scoped to their briefs. For new generated media, consult current owner decisions and have Vita inspect references/prompts and outputs.
- Check current availability, price, margin and service terms before offers. Do not invent scarcity, testimonials, discounts, bundles, guarantees, claim substantiation or customer demographics.
- Use live Shopify order/refund evidence or authorized exports for revenue. Vercel cart activity and external checkout clicks are not purchases. Define currency, reporting window, refunds and attribution window. Do not add platform-reported conversions together as total sales.
- Paid plans need known budget and viable contribution economics. A source benchmark is not a PASAJ target. Mark unavailable metrics as unknown; low traffic may make A/B testing impractical.
- Reverify current search/platform claims against primary documentation before implementation. The repository’s integration guides and CLI files do not establish installed tools, connected accounts, or permission to use them. Inspect any helper before executing it.
- Nico owns lifecycle audience, triggers, cadence, exclusions and service truth; Lauren owns the writing. Email/SMS timing and discounts remain proposals until justified by consent, customer evidence and economics.
- Creator/partner work requires accurate usage rights and relationship descriptions. Never imply endorsement, a partnership or permission to reuse assets without evidence.
