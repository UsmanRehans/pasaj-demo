# A Quiet Passage campaign

This folder contains the three approved PASAJ campaign concept stills and the finished 12-second vertical social advertisement.

- `material-orbit-wood.png` — Wood concept still
- `scent-as-trace-tobacco.png` — Tobacco concept still
- `quiet-refraction-rose-ottoman.png` — Rose Ottoman concept still
- `a-quiet-passage-social-ad.mp4` — final Instagram and Facebook advertisement

The stills were developed from PASAJ product references and the final animation was rendered in Higgsfield. Confirm all packaging microcopy against current production artwork before paid publication.
